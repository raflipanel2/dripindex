Privacy policy

How the site handles data, advertising and local notes.

Effective date

Effective 11 November 2026. DripIndex is a static educational site. We do not operate user accounts, newsletter signup forms or a server-side database for the calculator inputs and meter logs in this release.

Local browser storage

The Water Reading Log uses localStorage in your browser to save entries and may fail in private mode or when storage is blocked. These entries remain on your device unless you export them. You can delete them using the Clear button or browser-site-data controls. We do not send this log to our server.

Third-party advertising

This site includes the Google AdSense script on selected editorial pages to support publisher verification and potential ad delivery. Google and its partners may use cookies, identifiers and information according to their own policies when ads are served. Review Google’s advertising technology information. Depending on visitor geography, consent requirements and Google-certified consent management may apply; site operators should configure these before enabling ads where required.

Infrastructure and outside sites

Static hosting, delivery networks and linked third-party sites may process technical requests such as IP addresses as part of providing their services. External links use their own policies. For questions about this policy, write to raflipanel2@gmail.com.
