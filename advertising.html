Independence

DripIndex is an independent educational project. In this release, editorial recommendations are not paid placements and no product manufacturer has sponsored the featured guides. Future sponsorships, if any, should be separately and clearly labeled.

Google AdSense

AdSense verification code is included on the homepage and editorial guide pages. Google may decide whether any ads appear, and ad display is not a certification of our methods. We do not encourage clicking ads or adjusting a water-use result to attract an advertisement.

User-first placement

Calculator-only pages, contact/legal pages and guide-index pages intentionally do not load the AdSense script in this build. The purpose is to keep pages centered on actual publisher content rather than create ad-only screens.
