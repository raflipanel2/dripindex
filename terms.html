Terms of use

Important limitations for observations and calculations.

Educational use only

The site provides generalized explanations, estimates and planning checklists, not professional inspections, legal advice, safety clearances or guarantees of savings. Actual billing, fixture compatibility and local regulations must be checked independently.

Safe use

Do not tamper with water meters, seals, electrical equipment, pressurized pipes, water heaters or unknown valves based on an online example. If a test is not safe or a leak is actively damaging a property, contact local maintenance or qualified help.

Accuracy and changes

We aim for useful, accurate information as of the dates shown, but instructions, water tariffs, product specifications and service policies can change. Formulas use assumptions that should not be represented as precise measurements.
