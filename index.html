WHAT WE BELIEVE

Measure before you replace.

Not every higher bill means a leak. Not every droplet is a pipe failure. And no one savings percentage fits every household. Our field notes favor transparent observations and manufacturer or utility instructions over confident guesses.

REFERENCE / 001One drip is not a fixed volume.

USGS uses 0.25 mL as an illustrative faucet drop. Our calculator keeps that assumption editable rather than presenting an estimate as a meter reading.
