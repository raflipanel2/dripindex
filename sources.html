Primary sources

Technical references and scope notes.

U.S. EPA WaterSense

The EPA WaterSense program publishes leak-detection and fixture-efficiency guidance. We use it for observation methods such as the toilet dye test, the no-use water meter test and program-specific product criteria; we do not present U.S. criteria as universal standards.

U.S. Geological Survey

The USGS Water Science School explains the assumption behind illustrative drip-loss estimates, including the use of 0.25 milliliter as an example droplet volume. Our drip tool allows readers to change that assumption.

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention

CDC moisture and mold guidance supports the general advice to dry wet areas promptly and address the source of water. It does not replace professional remediation following sewage backup or major flooding.

Water and energy equipment

For decisions involving water heaters or pressure lines, follow the specific equipment manufacturer, local utility and qualified professional; broad online instructions are not a substitute for local code compliance.
