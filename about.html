About DripIndex

A field guide to measurable household water use.

Our purpose

DripIndex is an independent educational resource about practical household water-use observations. Its objective is to help readers turn vague concerns—an unusual bill, a damp cabinet or a dripping tap—into notes and measurements that can be discussed with a utility or qualified maintenance professional.

What this site is not

We are not a plumbing contractor, inspection company, laboratory, water utility or a manufacturer. We do not remotely diagnose a leak, certify a fixture, assess drinking-water quality or give directions to alter pressure pipes, meters or electrical systems. Readers should follow local rules and manufacturer instructions.

How the tools work

The calculators show their formulas and assumptions. The Water Reading Log stores entries in your browser’s local storage, when available. We do not operate a server-side account service for those notes. You can export or clear them yourself.

Editorial accountability

Each guide is dated and points to at least one relevant official primary source. We distinguish verified claims from illustrative scenarios. For factual errors, accessibility feedback or requests to correct a page, email raflipanel2@gmail.com. Our editorial standards explain how we maintain the site.
