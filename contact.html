Corrections, questions and accessibility feedback.

Email us

For corrections, source updates, accessibility issues or general inquiries, write to raflipanel2@gmail.com. Include the page URL and the specific sentence or calculation you are asking us to review.

What not to send

Please do not email water-account numbers, full bills, private addresses, passwords, payment details or photos containing personal identifying information. We cannot inspect an individual water system remotely or provide emergency plumbing services.

For urgent water problems

If water is flowing near electrical equipment, there is sewage contamination, or a leak is spreading rapidly, seek local emergency maintenance or qualified professional assistance immediately. This contact mailbox is not monitored as an emergency service.
